optimisation campaign was not rerun during packaging. Timings and environment
metadata are platform-dependent. Fine discrete Fréchet references approximate
continuous distance.

Fresh instance regeneration with `make bench` reproduced all 30 archived files
byte-for-byte. The run is recorded in `outputs/runs.jsonl` and took about 31
minutes on the validation machine. Archive verification and figure reproduction
use the supplied instances directly.

The selected research sources have no HPA imports. An older diagnostic with a
private HPA dependency was excluded together with its output. A static comparison
against 236 industrial source files found no shared normalised 12-line windows.
Pattern checks found no private keys, token signatures or local home paths in
the selected package. These checks establish the inspected boundary; ownership
and licensing remain the author's responsibility.

Commands append evidence to `outputs/runs.jsonl` and `outputs/audit/`. A first
figure-reproduction attempt exposed a missing output-directory creation in the
wrapper. That wrapper was corrected and the complete reanalysis then passed;
the failed attempt remains in the local append-only log.
